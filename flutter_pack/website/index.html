Compare price and duration, review each transfer and choose up to three connected transport legs.

Travel needs

Separate step-free access, wheelchair carriage and boarding assistance. Unknown required features exclude a result.

Manage

Know the limits

Transport schedules, driver records, tickets, prices and payment outcomes are synthetic. No actual payment is collected and no real vehicle is dispatched. The demo setting includes Aktobe and synthetic intercity services. This website is informational, not a booking client. App-store publication is not claimed.

Interface accessibility and transport suitability are separate. The prototype is not a certified accessibility or safety service.
